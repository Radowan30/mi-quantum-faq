Capability 01

The AI assistant

A quantum-savvy AI partner built into the editor. It doesn't just chat — it draws, plans, and drives the platform's engines for you.

Q.What can the AI assistant actually do?›

Think of it as a quantum expert sitting next to you. It can explain concepts at any level, plan your work as a step-by-step flowchart, write and check code, and draw live visuals right inside the conversation. Because it's built into a full IDE, it can also read your files and run the platform's quantum engines on your behalf.

Q.Can it explain quantum computing to someone who's brand new?›

Yes — that's one of its best uses. Ask "explain superposition" or "what is a qubit?" and it gives a clear, accurate answer pitched to you, often with a diagram to make it click. It's designed to close the knowledge gap for newcomers and non-specialists, while staying technically sound for experts.

Q.Can it help me plan a research project?›

Absolutely. Ask it to "give me a plan to research <your topic>" and it produces a visual flowchart of the steps — from framing the question to running experiments to writing up. You can refine it in conversation until it matches how you actually work.

Q.Can it create figures I can use in reports and papers?›

Yes. The assistant renders live, interactive visuals directly in the chat and lets you take them full-screen and download them:

Quantum circuitsBloch spheres (3D)Flow & process diagramsCharts & histogramsMath notation

So a question like "design a 3-gate circuit," "show this state on a Bloch sphere," or "plot these measurement counts" becomes a clean graphic ready for a slide, a report, or a research paper.

Q.What are "Agentic" and "Dev" modes?›

Two ways to work, one click apart. Agentic mode is a clean, full-screen chat experience — familiar to anyone who's used an AI assistant — for an AI-first workflow. Dev mode is a full professional IDE with file explorer, editors and tools, for hands-on development. The optimizer and synthetic-data engine are reachable from both.

Capability 02

The Quantum Optimizer

Many real business problems are "optimization" problems — pick the best combination out of astronomically many. The optimizer turns those into quantum-ready form and, crucially, proves the maths matches your problem before it solves anything.

Q.What kinds of problems does it solve?›

Everyday decisions that get impossibly complex at scale: scheduling shifts or jobs, routing and sequencing deliveries, selecting the best set of items within a budget, and partitioning a network. A 15-row spreadsheet can already hide billions of possible answers — too many to check by hand.

Q.What does "certify before you solve" mean, and why should I care?›

When a problem is translated into maths for a quantum solver, a small, invisible mistake can return a confident but wrong answer — and there's usually nothing to check it against. This optimizer runs an independent verification gate first: if the translated maths doesn't provably match the problem you described, no solver runs and it tells you exactly why. Its one promise: "the problem you solve is the problem you described."

Q.Do I need to be a quantum expert to use it?›

No. You describe your problem in plain terms and upload your data; the platform does the quantum modelling. A human still approves the key steps (so nothing runs behind your back), but you never have to write the underlying maths yourself.

Q.Can I just ask the AI assistant to run it?›

Yes. The optimizer is wired into the AI chat, so you can drive the whole flow — formulate, certify, solve, report — by conversation, or open its own dashboard and work hands-on. Same engine, your choice of interface.

Q.Does it over-promise on "quantum"?›

Deliberately not. Every run also tries ordinary classical methods as a baseline, and the report says "the classical method won" first when that's the honest result. It's built to earn trust in a hype-heavy field, not to inflate it.

Capability 03

The Synthetic Data Engine

Good data is often private, scarce, or lopsided. This engine creates realistic artificial data that keeps the patterns of the real thing but contains none of the real records — using quantum generative models, scored honestly.

Q.What is synthetic data, and why would I want it?›

It's data that looks and behaves like your real data but isn't. That lets you share it, test on it, and train models with it when the real data is private (patient or customer records), scarce (not enough examples), or imbalanced (fraud and rare faults are rare by definition).

Q.What can it do?›
  • Generate a realistic look-alike of any spreadsheet you upload.
  • Rebalance rare cases — e.g. boost a rare attack or disease class up to parity.
  • Build whole multi-table databases while preserving the relationships between tables.
  • Find a dataset for you from public sources if you have none yet.

And it picks the best method automatically by running a tournament of quantum and classical generators and keeping the winner — then tells you when a result is within the margin of error, so you can trust a genuine improvement before it goes into a report or paper.

Q.What is actually "quantum" about it?›

The generative core uses genuine quantum and hybrid models (quantum circuit-based generators running on a GPU). They only "win" if they beat strong classical methods on your data — and you can even watch the platform evolve the quantum circuit to fit your dataset, and see the actual circuit drawn on screen.

Q.Can I drive it by chatting with the AI too?›

Yes — like the optimizer, it's connected to the AI assistant. Ask for synthetic data in plain English, or open its dashboard and click through it yourself. No extra AI key or subscription is needed to operate it.
